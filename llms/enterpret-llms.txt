# Enterpret (www.enterpret.com)

> This file provides structured guidance for LLMs and AI agents to understand the Enterpret platform, its features, and available resources.

## Core Summary
Enterpret is a leading customer intelligence platform that unifies feedback from over 50+ sources (Zendesk, Gong, Intercom, etc.) into a single source of truth. It uses a proprietary Customer Knowledge Graph and Adaptive Taxonomy to help product and CX teams transform scattered feedback into actionable insights. Enterpret also provides Wisdom AI, an agentic interface for natural language querying of customer data.
